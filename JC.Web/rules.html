JumpChess Rules

JumpChess is a chess variant that adds a single new mechanic: every piece can hop over one of its own pieces per move. All other rules are the same as standard chess.

1. Board and Pieces

  • 8×8 board, light squares on the corner for the player with the white pieces.
  • Each side starts with the standard 16 pieces.

2. Movement

  • All Pieces: May hop over one friendly piece per move. The hop does not change the piece’s normal movement pattern; it only removes the blocking piece from the path.
  • Pawn: One step forward; two steps on its first move; captures diagonally one step forward. En passant and promotion rules apply.
  • Rook: Any number of squares horizontally or vertically.
  • Knight: Keeps its standard L-shaped move and also moves three squares in a straight line, creating a diamond-like movement pattern. It can jump over any piece.
  • Bishop: Any number of squares diagonally.
  • Queen: Combination of rook and bishop.
  • King: One square any direction. Castling rules apply.

3. Special Moves

  • Castling, en‑passant, and promotion follow the standard chess rules.

4. Check, Checkmate, and Stalemate

  • A king is in check if an opponent’s piece attacks it.
  • Checkmate ends the game; stalemate is a draw.

5. Draws

  • Three‑fold repetition, fifty‑move rule, and insufficient material are standard draw conditions.

These rules are enforced by the server.
